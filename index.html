What is the requirement and scope for CCIS?

Set up a complete, modern Robotics & Innovation Lab in our newest branch that serves 800+ students (Grades 1–8) during regular hours, with an after-school excellence track for high schoolers.

4 Vendors

Who are we choosing between?

UWR (Turnkey lab + 50:50 profit), Robofun Lab (World championships & student patents), My Equation (Physical AI & ROS), and Cubotics (LEGO play-way foundation).

Tri-Tier Hybrid Model: Partner with UWR for the branch lab (earns ₹95L profit & trainers supplied) + engage Robofun Lab for parent-funded international competition teams (FTC/FRC) to maximize school PR.

STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT

Puru's Recommendation & Action Roadmap

A smart, high-return strategy tailored for Cambridge Court's multi-branch scale:

STEP 1 • NEW BRANCH LAB

Deploy UWR Under the 50:50 Revenue Share Model

Install the turnkey lab in our newest branch for Grades 1 to 8. This eliminates teacher hiring issues because UWR provides dedicated trainers on their payroll, while our school earns ₹95+ Lakhs net profit annually.

STEP 2 • WORLD COMPETITIONS

Engage Robofun Lab for After-School Teams

Form a parent-sponsored elite CCIS FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) squad mentored by Ashwin Shah. This puts Cambridge Court on the global stage in Houston and produces student patents that power Ivy League admissions.

STEP 3 • ZERO-RISK TEST

Send CCIS Students to Free NASA Hackathon (Nov 14)

Register 20–30 CCIS students for UWR's free NASA Space Apps Hackathon in Jaipur on November 14. This lets us test their mentors and execution quality firsthand before signing any contract.
